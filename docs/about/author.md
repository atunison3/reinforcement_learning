# About the Author

Andy Tunison is a mechanical engineer and applied data scientist with a background in aerospace systems, avionics, and digital engineering. His work focuses on applying machine learning, data science, and automation to complex engineering problems.

He holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and an M.S. in Applied Data Science from the University of Michigan. His technical interests include machine learning, reinforcement learning, artificial intelligence, reliability engineering, and the application of data-driven methods to aerospace systems.

This repository documents his ongoing study and implementation of reinforcement learning concepts, with an emphasis on understanding the underlying theory and translating it into practical Python implementations.
